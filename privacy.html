Metrônomo Progressivo privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Metrônomo Progressivo has no sign-up, login or account. The app never asks for your name, email or any other personal data. This page explains what is kept on your device and what is collected by the Google services the app uses to show ads and find bugs.

What stays on your device

Your last session settings (start and max BPM, increment, time signature and end behavior) are saved in the app's internal storage so you don't have to set everything up again. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and system information, and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before any ad is loaded, and the “Privacy options” link on the home screen lets you review that choice at any time.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android settings (Google › Ads). Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Firebase Analytics records aggregated information about how the app is used, such as screens opened, session length, device model, Android version, language and approximate country. This data is used to understand and improve the app and does not identify you by name.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a technical report with where in the code the error happened, the device model, the Android version and the app's state at the time. Reports are used only to fix bugs.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: load ads and send statistics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob, as described above.
  • Notifications and foreground service: keep the metronome playing with the screen off and show the playback notification. No data is collected through them.

Sharing

The data described above is processed by Google, as the operator of AdMob and Firebase, under the Google Privacy Policy. The app's developer does not sell or share any data with other companies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Brazil's LGPD, you may request information about, correction of, or deletion of your data. Since the app doesn't know who you are, the most direct way is to reset the advertising ID in Android settings and clear the app's data. For anything else, write to the contact below.
